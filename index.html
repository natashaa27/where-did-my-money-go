FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Mosaic?

Mosaic is a financial intelligence agent. It brings all your bank accounts, cards and UPI apps into one place and lets you ask questions about your money in plain language — like talking to a personal analyst who has read every statement you've ever had.

Can Mosaic move my money?

No. Mosaic only has read-only access to your financial data. It can analyse, explain and alert — it cannot make payments or transfers.

How does Mosaic connect to my bank?

Through RBI's Account Aggregator framework. You give explicit, time-bound consent for each account, and you can revoke it at any time from the app.

Is the AI giving me financial advice?

Mosaic gives you insights based on your own data — spending patterns, affordability checks, savings pace. It isn't a registered investment advisor and won't recommend specific securities.

How much does it cost?

Mosaic is free during the beta. Early waitlist members will get founding-member pricing when paid plans launch.
